Skool IRL Meetups

A laid-back way for local Skool members, founders, and digital builders in Chattanooga to connect in real life.

We meet up at local spots, grab coffee or food, talk shop, share ideas, and build real connections outside the internet.

Join the Skool Community

What It’s About

This isn’t a formal conference or a stiff networking event.

It’s a relaxed meetup for people building things online — Skool communities, digital products, e-commerce brands, content platforms, and more.

If you’re creating, growing, or experimenting in the digital world, you’re welcome.

What to Expect

  • Casual conversations about what you’re building
  • Sharing wins, challenges, and ideas
  • Meeting other local digital creators
  • Real friendships — not just LinkedIn connections

Who Should Come

Anyone building online.

Whether you’re just getting started or already running something serious, this is a space to connect with other like-minded people in Chattanooga.

No pressure. No pitch fest. Just good conversations and real community.
